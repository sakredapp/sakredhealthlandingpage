I need you to refactor the UI/UX branding for "Sakred Health" into a "Premium Organic" aesthetic. We are pivoting away from Dark Mode. The new design system is built around a **Vanilla Cream** base, evoking feelings of natural paper, calmness, and clarity.

**Global Design Variables & Theme:**

1.  **Backgrounds (The "Vanilla" Base):**
    * **Main BG:** `#FDFBF7` (Warm Vanilla/Cream).
    * **Secondary BG:** `#F6F4EF` (Slightly darker Stone/Paper for distinct sections).
    * **Avoid:** Stark white (`#ffffff`) as a page background.

2.  **Typography & Contrast (Crucial Fixes):**
    * **Primary Text:** `#1C1917` (Warm Black/Stone 900) - Used for headings.
    * **Body Text:** `#44403C` (Stone 700) - Softer than black for readability on cream.
    * **Accent Text:** `#0E7490` (Deep Teal) - Used for links and active states to ensure accessible contrast against cream.

3.  **Accent Palette:**
    * **Primary Brand Color:** `#06B6D4` (Cyan) is too light for text on cream. Use `#0891B2` (Teal 600) for buttons and primary actions.
    * **Glows/Highlights:** Use `#67E8F9` (Cyan 300) ONLY for background blurs, gradients, or low-opacity decorative elements, never for text.
    * **Success/Harmony:** `#14B8A6` (Teal 500).

4.  **UI Physics & Texture:**
    * **Glassmorphism (Light Mode):** Instead of dark glass, use "Frosted Ice."
        * `bg-white/60 backdrop-blur-md border border-white/40 shadow-sm`.
    * **Shadows:** Do not use black shadows. Use colored diffused shadows for an elevated feel.
        * Example: `shadow-[0_8px_30px_rgb(0,0,0,0.04)]` or warm shadows `shadow-orange-500/5`.
    * **Borders:** Subtle, organic borders. `border-stone-200` or `border-stone-900/5`.
