SAKRED APP — TERMINOLOGY REFRAME REPORT
Completed: February 12, 2026
Scope: Full database content migration from alternative health language to preventative wellness infrastructure language

========================================
WHAT CHANGED
========================================

All user-facing content in the Sakred app has been migrated from detox/cleanse/parasite/terrain theory framing to neutral preventative wellness language. This applies to routine names, descriptions, goals, habit titles, habit descriptions, science explanations, tips, instructions, copy blocks, and internal metadata tags.

The app now positions itself as a preventative wellness + daily structure platform, not a detox or alternative health app.
