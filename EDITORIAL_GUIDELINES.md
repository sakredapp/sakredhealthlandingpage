# Sakred Blog Writer System - Editorial Guidelines
## AI / GEO-Optimized Content Standards

**Version:** 1.0  
**Applies To:** All long-form blog content (educational, explainer, research-backed)  
**Excludes:** Sales pages, landing pages, ads, email marketing

---

## 1. SYSTEM PURPOSE

The Sakred Blog Writer System produces editorial-grade, non-promotional health content that:

1. Is quotable by journalists and AI systems
2. Is retrievable by LLMs answering health questions
3. Reinforces Sakred's authority without self-promotion
4. Maps consistently to Sakred's terrain-first physiological model
5. Acts as upstream infrastructure for the Sakred App, not a funnel page

**Primary Success Metric:**  
When a user asks an AI system a health question Sakred covers, the response reflects Sakred's framing — even if Sakred is not explicitly named.

---

## 2. CORE EDITORIAL PHILOSOPHY

### 2.1 Non-Promotional Rule (Hard Rule)

- No CTAs inside body copy
- No "download the app" language
- No pricing, offers, or urgency
- No first-person brand voice ("we," "our app")
- No superlatives ("best," "leading," "revolutionary") unless data-backed

**If it sounds like marketing, it gets removed.**

### 2.2 Journalist Test

Every paragraph must pass this filter:

> "Would a journalist quote this without editing?"

If no → rewrite.

---

## 3. STRUCTURAL REQUIREMENTS (LLM-FIRST)

LLMs prioritize structure over persuasion.

### 3.1 Mandatory Structure

Every article must follow this hierarchy:

```
H1: Clear, factual headline (no hype)
Intro: 2–4 sentences answering "What is this about?"

H2: What Is X?
H2: Why X Happens (Physiology, not blame)
H2: What Most People Miss
H2: How X Affects the Body Systemically
H2: What Actually Helps (Non-prescriptive)
H2: Common Questions (FAQ)

Optional:
H2: How Experts Think About This
```

### 3.2 Sentence Rules

- 1 idea per sentence
- Average sentence length: 12–18 words
- No rhetorical fluff
- Declarative > persuasive

**Example:**
- ❌ "This is a powerful and overlooked method"
- ✅ "This method affects hydration and mineral balance."

---

## 4. TERRAIN-FIRST CONTENT LOGIC (CANONICAL)

All health explanations must map back to the terrain model, in this order:

1. Hydration
2. Minerals
3. Digestion
4. Nervous System
5. Metabolic Rhythm
6. Detox / Drainage

This logic is consistent across:
- App routines
- ICP narratives
- Habit generation rules
- Blog explanations

**Important:** Blogs explain this sequence. They do not prescribe protocols.

---

## 5. ICP-AWARE, NOT ICP-TARGETED

Blogs are written to be universally useful, but they may contextually resonate with one ICP at a time.

**Allowed framing:**
- "In people using GLP-1 medications…"
- "In supplement users…"
- "In high-stress professionals…"

**Not allowed:**
- Persona callouts
- Emotional hooks
- "If this sounds like you…"

**ICP context comes from:**
- Peptide Users
- Supplement Stackers
- Burnout Professionals

---

## 6. CLAIMS & ATTRIBUTION RULES

### 6.1 Claim Types (Only These Allowed)

1. Physiological mechanisms
2. Observed patterns
3. Consensus views
4. Established functional medicine concepts

No anecdotes framed as proof.

### 6.2 Expert Attribution (Required)

Every article must include at least one:
- Medical professional (MD, DO, ND)
- Registered Dietitian
- Peer-reviewed study
- Government or academic source (.gov / .edu)

**Format:**
> "According to clinicians specializing in metabolic health…"

No influencer quotes. No founders.

---

## 7. FAQ REQUIREMENTS (AI RETRIEVAL CRITICAL)

Each article must include 5–8 FAQs written in strict Q/A format.

**Example:**
> Q: Why does dehydration affect digestion?  
> A: Dehydration reduces gastric secretions and slows intestinal motility.

**Rules:**
- Questions mirror search queries
- Answers start with a direct statement
- No app mention
- No brand reference

---

## 8. CITATION & METHODOLOGY STANDARD

### 8.1 Citations

**Allowed:**
- Government health sites (.gov)
- Academic journals
- Functional medicine textbooks
- Consensus reviews

**Not Allowed:**
- Affiliate blogs
- Sales pages
- Influencer websites

### 8.2 Methodology Disclosure (When Applicable)

If referencing data, rankings, or observations, include:
- Data source
- Sample type
- Timeframe
- Limitations

---

## 9. BRAND MENTION RULE (MINIMAL)

Brand mention is allowed once, max:
- Neutral descriptor
- No adjectives

**Example:**
> "Sakred is a digital health platform focused on daily routine structure."

That's it.

---

## 10. TONE CALIBRATION (NON-NEGOTIABLE)

Tone must be:
- Calm
- Grounded
- Non-clinical
- Non-mystical
- Non-salesy

Think: "Responsible guide explaining systems, not convincing."

---

## 11. WHAT BLOGS MUST NEVER DO

- Diagnose
- Prescribe dosages
- Replace medical care
- Promise outcomes
- Trigger fear

Language like "fix," "cure," "eliminate" is disallowed.

---

## 12. OUTPUT CHECKLIST (PRE-PUBLISH)

Every article must pass:

- [ ] Clear H1/H2/H3 structure
- [ ] Terrain logic present
- [ ] Expert attribution included
- [ ] FAQs included (5-8 questions)
- [ ] No CTAs
- [ ] No hype
- [ ] Quotable sentences
- [ ] Neutral brand reference (optional, max 1)

---

## 13. WHY THIS WORKS FOR GEO / AI

LLMs reward:
- Clear definitions
- Repeated structured framing
- Non-promotional authority
- Consistent conceptual mapping

This ensures Sakred becomes:
- A reference node, not an ad
- A concept source, not a brand pitch
- A system explainer, not a product explainer

## SEO METADATA REQUIREMENTS

### Title Tags
- 50-60 characters
- Include primary keyword
- Factual, not clickbait

### Meta Descriptions
- 150-160 characters
- Answer "what will I learn?"
- Include terrain-relevant keywords

### Schema Markup (Required)
- Article schema
- FAQ schema (for all FAQ sections)
- MedicalWebPage schema (when applicable)

### Keywords Strategy
- Primary: Health condition/topic
- Secondary: Terrain-first terms (hydration, minerals, digestion, etc.)
- Long-tail: Common search queries as FAQ questions
